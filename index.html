How to Play QuizMaster

Quick rules and gameplay instructions

1

Select Your Domain & Name

Enter your player name and pick one of the 6 curated categories. Each game randomly selects 10 questions with shuffled choices.

2

Answer Against the Timer

You have 2 minutes and 30 seconds (150s) to complete all 10 questions. If the clock hits 00:00, your quiz will automatically submit!

3

Navigate Freely Without Score Duplication

Each question allows one answer selection. You can click Previous Question to review your earlier picks without losing or duplicating points.

4

Analyze Results & Compete

View your final percentage, review detailed explanations for every question, and check your rank on the leaderboard!

Pro Tip: Keyboard Navigation

You can use keys 1, 2, 3, 4 (or A, B, C, D) to select answers swiftly, and Enter to proceed to the next question.
